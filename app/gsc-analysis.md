## 3. Risk created by the redesign

The old PT home was built around Europass: title "Curriculum Vitae Europass Grátis – Criar CV Profissional |
Curriculum Vitae Editar", "Europass" ×80 on the page. The new PT home title is "Fazer currículo no iPhone…",
and "Europass" now appears only in the FAQ (×11). **This page delivered 50 of the site's 102 clicks.**
Expect the PT Europass rankings to drop unless the topic gets a dedicated page.

The same applies on a smaller scale:
- IT (old title "Curriculum Vitae Europeo Gratis") — rankings were weak anyway (pos 45–65).
- FR (old title "CV Designer Gratuit… France Travail") — dropped on purpose: competitor brand, pos 26, 1 click in 90 days.

The old titles also over-promised ("Europass grátis"): the app does not create the official Europass CV, and PDF
export is premium. A new page must stay honest.

## 4. Recommendations (by priority)

1. **PT — new guide `/pt/guias/editar-cv-europass/`** for the edit cluster (5.4k impressions, already at pos ~10).
   Title: "Como editar o CV Europass (atualizar e alterar): passo a passo". It explains how to edit and update the
   CV on the official Europass site (upload the PDF/XML, edit, download), common problems, and the alternative: a
   European-style CV on iPhone with CV Builder. It is honest that the app is not Europass.
2. **PT — strengthen `curriculo-europass`**: retitle to "Curriculum vitae Europass e modelo europeu: como criar
   grátis" and add an H2 "Currículo modelo europeu" plus a "grátis" FAQ. Target the "modelo europeu" (2.8k) and
   "grátis" (4.8k) clusters.
3. **PT home — bring "Europass / modelo europeu" back into the meta description and one hero line**, and link
   both guides from the home FAQ and the guides block. Keep the title iPhone-first. Do not claim "Europass grátis".
4. **RU — CTR**: positions 3–5 with a CTR near 0. The new title already contains "на iPhone"; the
   `kak-sostavit-rezyume-na-iphone` guide should carry the exact phrasing "как создать резюме на айфоне"
   (title/H1 variant). Re-check in 4 weeks.
5. **PL — `cv-na-telefonie`**: add "na iPhone" to the title ("Jak zrobić CV na iPhonie / telefonie") to match
   "jak zrobic cv na iphone" (pos 9.4).
6. **IT — reuse the PT approach** (`curriculum-europeo` + an "modificare cv europass" section) only after PT
   proves it works; current positions are too low to be quick wins.
7. **Brazil (3k impressions)**: the PT copy is PT-PT ("telemóvel", "descarregar"). A pt-BR variant is a later,
   separate project.
8. **Ignore**: "cvbuilder" brand look-alikes, "CV Designer / France Travail", Japanese "履歴書 簡単" (the app does
   not make the Japanese rirekisho form).
9. **Re-run `npm run gsc` around 2026-11-01** (4 weeks on the new template, guides indexed) and compare against this baseline.
