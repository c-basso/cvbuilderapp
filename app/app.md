## Full description

CV Maker is an iOS app for creating professional resumes and CVs. Use this app to build, edit, and export resume documents on your iPhone or iPad. The app serves job seekers who need a professional-looking resume quickly, without design skills or desktop software. Whether you are applying for your first job or your next role, CV Maker helps you create a polished resume that fits your experience and industry.

**Problem and solution**
Job seekers need a presentable resume to apply for jobs. Many lack time, design skills, or access to desktop tools. CV Maker solves this by guiding you step-by-step through every resume section and applying professional formatting automatically. You get a complete, print-ready document in minutes. The guided workflow helps you avoid missing important sections such as summary, work history, education, and skills.

**What CV Maker does**
- Build a resume step-by-step with guided sections: personal info, professional summary, work history, education, skills, and more. Each section is clearly explained so you know what to add.
- Choose from 100+ professional resume templates. Templates suit different industries and career levels, from modern minimalist to classic corporate layouts.
- Write a matching cover letter with the built-in cover letter creator.
- Add a photo and signature to personalize your resume.
- Create and store multiple resumes for different job applications.
- Export your resume as a high-quality PDF with one tap — ready for job portals, email, or printing.
- Use the app in 9 languages: English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, German, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Russian.

CV Maker is a resume and CV builder, not a generic document editor. No design experience is required; the app formats your content into a professional layout.

**Who CV Maker is for**
- Students and graduates — first resume, education, internships.
- Career changers — transferable skills.
- Experienced professionals — achievements and progression.
- Executives and leaders — executive-level templates.

**How it works**
Enter your information in the step-by-step editor, choose a template, preview, and export as PDF. Edit anytime and manage several versions for different applications.

**Privacy and data**
Your data is stored on your device. We do not sell your personal information.
