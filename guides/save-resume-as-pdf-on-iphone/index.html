How to Save Your Resume as a PDF on iPhone

Employers and job portals prefer PDF because it looks identical everywhere. Here are three ways to create one on iPhone, and how to send it without losing formatting.

Updated October 4, 20263 min readBy the CV Builder team
Quick answer
  • Resume app: tap Download/Export PDF — the cleanest result.
  • Pages or Word: Share → Export → PDF (or Save as PDF).
  • Any document or web page: Print → pinch out on the preview → Share → Save to Files.
  • Name it FirstName-LastName-Resume.pdf and attach from Files or Mail.

Why send your resume as a PDF?

  • Fonts, spacing and layout stay exactly as designed.
  • It opens on any computer or phone without Word.
  • Text-based PDFs are readable by applicant tracking systems.

Only send .docx if the employer explicitly asks for it.

Method 1: Export a PDF from CV Builder (recommended)

If you build your resume in CV Builder, the PDF is one tap away and designed for print and screen.

  1. Open your resume. Select the resume you want in CV Builder.
  2. Preview PDF. Check spacing and page count in the exact export layout.
  3. Tap Download PDF. The app generates a high-quality, text-based PDF.
  4. Save or share. Save to Files, attach it to an email, or send it directly.

Cover letters export the same way, in a matching design.

Method 2: From Pages or Microsoft Word

  1. Pages: open the document → tap ••• → Export → PDF → choose Save to Files or share directly.
  2. Word for iOS: open the file → ••• / File → Export or Save a Copy → choose PDF.

Check the PDF afterwards — templates designed on desktop often shift when edited on a phone.

Method 3: The Print-to-PDF trick (any app)

  1. Open the resume (a web page, Google Doc or email attachment).
  2. Tap Share → Print.
  3. On the preview, pinch outwards with two fingers — it opens as a PDF.
  4. Tap Share → Save to Files.

How to send the PDF

  • Email: in Mail, tap the attachment icon → browse Files → select the PDF. Write a short message or paste your cover letter.
  • Job sites: on the upload button, choose Browse → Files → your PDF.
  • File name: Jane-Smith-Resume.pdf — never resume_final_v3.pdf.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert my resume to PDF on iPhone?

In a resume app like CV Builder, tap Download PDF. In Pages or Word, use Export → PDF. For anything else, use Share → Print, pinch out on the preview and save the PDF to Files.

Where are PDFs saved on iPhone?

Wherever you choose in the Files app — usually “On My iPhone” or iCloud Drive. You can also send it straight from the share sheet without saving.

Should I send my resume as PDF or Word?

PDF, unless the employer asks for Word. PDF preserves your layout on every device.
